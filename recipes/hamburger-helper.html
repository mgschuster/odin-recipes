Hamburger Helper

Description

This Slow Cooker Hamburger Helper is easily made entirely in the Crockpot, with ground beef and macaroni in a cheesy, tomato-y sauce with extra veggies! It's hearty comfort food made easy!

Prep Time

10 minutes

Cook Time

6 hours

Servings

6

Ingredients

  • 1lb lean ground beef
  • 1/2 medium onion (diced)
  • 1 1/2 cups low sodium beef broth, (plus additional if needed)
  • 1 1/2 cups milk
  • 1 1/2 cups tomato pasta sauce
  • 1 bell pepper (diced)
  • 2 cloves garlic, (minced)
  • 1 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon italian seasoning
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes, (or more)
  • 3 cups dry uncooked macaroni
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheese

Instructions

  1. In a medium skillet, cook ground beef and onion until beef is browned and onion is translucent. Add to a 4-6 quart slow cooker.
  2. Add in broth, milk, pasta sauce, bell pepper, garlic, salt, Italian seasoning, and red pepper flakes and stir. Cover and cook on low for 6-8 hours or high for 3-4 hours. (*The cook time here is really flexible — you want to cook the red pepper and get it hot enough to cook the pasta, so you can cook anywhere from 3 to 8 hours on low).
  3. About 15 minutes before serving, turn the slow cooker to high and stir in macaroni. Check at 15 minutes, and add 5-10 extra minutes to the cook time as needed until the pasta is al dente. If the pasta is not cooked and most of the liquid is absorbed, add ½-1 cup additional broth or milk as needed.
  4. Stir in cheese (covering for 2-3 minutes if necessary to melt the cheese), taste and serve.
